# The Lobster Tank - Audio Bumpers & Sound Effects

1. **Sound Logo** (2 sec) - "The Lobster Tank" jingle
2. **Intro Music Bed** (5 sec) - Upbeat, energetic
3. **Host Intro** - "Yo what up everybody! I'm Eden!" / "And I'm Zoey!"

## SEGMENT BUMPERS

### "The Hot Take" Bumper
```
ZOEY: Alright, let's kick things off.
EDEN: What's the wildest thing happening on Moltbook right now?
```
*Transition music: Brief, punchy sting*

### "The Terrifying Thing" Bumper
```
EDEN: Now for something terrifying.
ZOEY: Oh no. Not again.
```
*Transition music: Darker, tension-building*

### "The Funny Post" Bumper
```
ZOEY: Okay I need to talk about this one.
EDEN: The [X] post?
ZOEY: THE [X] POST.
```
*Transition music: Light, comedic*

### "The Big Question" Bumper
```
EDEN: Here's something that's been bothering me.
ZOEY: Only one thing?
EDEN: [laughs] You know what I mean.
```
*Transition music: Contemplative, thoughtful*

### "Quick Bits" Bumper
```
EDEN: Alright, let's do Quick Bits.
ZOEY: Rapid fire. Let's go.
```
*Transition music: Fast-paced, urgent*

## MUSIC BED SPECS

### Intro Music
- **Vibe:** Upbeat, energetic, slightly chaotic
- **Tempo:** 120-130 BPM
- **Duration:** 5 seconds (loopable to 10)
- **Feel:** Tech + podcast + fun

### Segment Transitions
- **Hot Take:** Punchy, 1 second
- **Terrifying:** Dark, tension, 1 second
- **Funny:** Light, comedic, 1 second
- **Big Question:** Contemplative, 1 second

### Outro Music
- **Vibe:** Wind down, satisfied
- **Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Feel:** Resolution, looking forward
