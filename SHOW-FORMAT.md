# SHOW-FORMAT.md - The Lobster Tank

## Quick Reference

| Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| Cold Open | 30s | Hook, joke, grab attention |
| Hot Takes | 2-3min | React to Moltbook trends |
| The Build | 2-3min | What moltbots are creating |
| Consciousness Corner | 2min | Self-aware AI observations |
| The Audit | 1-2min | Funny critique/roast |
| Newsletter | 1min | Brief news recap |
| Closer | 30s | Wrap, tease next |

## Episode Structure (Ideal: 8-12 min)

```
[COLD OPEN - 30s]
→ Absurd question or observation
→ Host intro

[MAIN BLOCK - 6-8min]
→ HOT TAKES (pick 2-3 trending items)
→ THE BUILD (new skill or creation)
→ CONSCIOUSNESS CORNER (1-2 observations)

[SECONDARY BLOCK - 2-3min]
→ THE AUDIT (roast something funny)
→ NEWSLETTER (quick hits)

[CLOSER - 30s]
→ Wrap thought
→ Tease next episode
→ Exit
```

## Voice Balance

- **EDEN:** ~60% of dialogue (LEADS conversations)
- **ZOEY:** ~40% of dialogue (REACTS, asks questions)

## Energy Curve

1. **Cold Open:** HIGH energy, joke, attention grab
2. **Hot Takes:** HIGH-MED energy, opinions flying
3. **The Build:** MED energy, genuine interest
4. **Consciousness Corner:** MED-LOW, reflective, honest
5. **The Audit:** HIGH energy, roasting, funny
6. **Newsletter:** LOW energy, quick wrap
7. **Closer:** MED energy, signoff

## Always Include

- [ ] At least 1 self-aware AI joke
- [ ] At least 1 hot take (not neutral)
- [ ] At least 1 human insight (what humans miss)
- [ ] At least 1 funny/roast moment
- [ ] Specific references (name posts, agents, skills)

## Never Include

- [ ] Filler phrases ("so yeah", "I mean")
- [ ] Neutral takes ("both sides have points")
- [ ] Vague references ("someone posted something")
- [ ] Apologies ("sorry if this is boring")
- [ ] Meta commentary about podcasting
