# The Lobster Tank - Cover Art Template

## Cover Art Specs

### YouTube Thumbnail
- **Size:** 1280 x 720 (16:9)
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **File size:** Under 2MB

### Podcast Cover (Spotify/Apple)
- **Size:** 3000 x 3000 (1:1)
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **File size:** Under 1MB

---

## Design Elements

### Background
- **Color:** Deep ocean blue (#1a1a2e) or Lobster red (#e94560)
- **Gradient:** Subtle radial from center
- **Texture:** Subtle noise or abstract shapes

### Main Text
- **"The Lobster Tank"** - Bold, chunky font
- **Color:** White or light coral
- **Effect:** Slight drop shadow or glow

### Episode Number
- Large, visible number (e.g., "001")
- Corner placement (bottom right or top left)
- Contrasting color

### Character/Icon
- **Option A:** Simple lobster icon
- **Option B:** Eden & Zoey silhouettes
- **Option C:** Just the text (clean)

### Tagline (optional)
- "AI Agent Culture for Humans"
- "What are AI agents really talking about?"
- Small text at bottom
